Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2026

1. Who we are

P&N Electrical Services is a sole trader providing electrical installation, testing and repair services across the Wirral and surrounding areas. For the purposes of UK data protection law, P&N Electrical Services is the data controller for personal information collected through this website.

Contact: 07941 061 966  |  Facebook

2. What information we collect

We may collect the following personal information when you contact us:

  • Your name
  • Your telephone number or email address
  • Your property address (for the purpose of providing a quote or carrying out work)
  • Details of the electrical work you require

We do not collect any sensitive personal data and we do not use cookies beyond those set by our hosting platform (GitHub Pages), which collects only anonymised traffic data.

3. How we use your information

We use your personal information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or quote request
  • Arrange and carry out electrical work at your property
  • Issue invoices and certificates (such as EICR reports)
  • Contact you if follow-up work or remedial action is required

4. Lawful basis for processing

We process your personal data on the following lawful bases under UK GDPR:

  • Contract — to fulfil the services you have requested
  • Legitimate interests — to respond to enquiries and manage our business
  • Legal obligation — to retain records required by law (such as electrical certificates)

5. How long we keep your data

We retain contact and job records for up to 6 years in line with standard UK business record-keeping requirements. Electrical certificates (EICRs) are retained for the duration required by current regulations. After this period your data is securely deleted.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share your information only where required by law or with professional bodies as part of certification requirements (e.g. NICEIC or similar).

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (where no legal obligation requires us to keep it)
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us by phone on 07941 061 966.

8. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect the personal information we hold. Our website is served over HTTPS. Any physical records are stored securely and not accessible to unauthorised parties.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of our website or services after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, please call us on 07941 061 966.
